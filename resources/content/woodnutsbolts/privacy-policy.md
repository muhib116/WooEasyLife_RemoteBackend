# Privacy Policy — Wood Nuts & Bolts

**Last updated:** 12 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the mobile game **Wood Nuts & Bolts** (the “App”) on Google Play.

---

## 1. Summary

The App is a single-player puzzle game. We do not require you to create an account. Progress is stored on your device. The App may show ads and offer an optional in-app purchase to remove interstitial ads. Advertising and analytics partners may collect device and usage data as described below.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 On your device

- Game progress (level, settings, undo charges, remove-ads entitlement)
- Audio / haptics preferences

This data stays on your device unless you use a future cloud-save feature (not required for the current version).

### 2.2 Advertising

If ads are enabled, we use Google AdMob (and possibly mediation partners). These services may collect:

- Advertising ID / device identifiers
- IP address and approximate location
- Device model, OS version, app version
- Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

See Google’s policy: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 2.3 Analytics & crash reporting

If enabled, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may collect:

- App events (e.g. level start/clear, ad events)
- Crash logs and performance diagnostics
- Device and app version information

Firebase privacy: [Firebase Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### 2.4 In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card details. Google’s purchase records may confirm that you own “Remove Ads”.

---

## 3. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own profiling. Ad and analytics SDKs may still operate under their own policies and consent frameworks (including UMP where required).

---

## 4. Consent & your choices

- Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), we present a consent form before personalized ads.
- You can reset or limit your advertising ID in device settings.
- You may purchase Remove Ads to stop interstitial ads (rewarded optional features may remain).
- You can mute sound in the App settings.

---

## 5. Data retention

Local game data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall. Ad/analytics retention follows the policies of Google / Firebase.

---

## 6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by service providers (Google Play, AdMob, Firebase) solely to operate ads, analytics, crashes, and purchases.

---

## 7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the App. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

---

## 8. International transfers

Service providers may process data on servers outside your country. Their safeguards apply as described in their policies.

---

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
