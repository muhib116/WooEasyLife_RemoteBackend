# Terms of Service — Courier Check

**Effective date:** 1 November 2026  
**Last updated:** 1 November 2026

## 1. The service

Courier Check is a free WordPress plugin from WPSaleHub. It lets a WooCommerce store send a customer billing phone number and receive courier delivery history for that number.

There is no account and no fee for the free daily allowance. Creating a site key does not create a WPSaleHub user account.

**Contact:** dev.muhibbullah@gmail.com  
**Website:** [WPSaleHub](https://app.wpsalehub.com)

## 2. The free daily allowance

Each site key can use the same free daily allowance as the public checker on this website. The allowance also applies to the site’s domain, so creating another key for the same domain does not start the allowance over.

When the allowance is used up, the check waits until the next midnight in Asia/Dhaka. We may refuse or revoke a site key that is used to get around this limit.

## 3. What the result means

The result is courier delivery history and a risk label:

- **low** — delivery success in the same “good history” band the public checker shows
- **medium** — the public checker’s middle band
- **high** — the public checker’s lower band
- **unknown** — no delivery history was found

The label is not a statement that an order or a person is genuine, and it is not a statement that an order or a person is not genuine. Courier data can be missing or delayed. You decide what to do with the result.

## 4. Your use of the plugin

You may use the plugin on a WordPress site you operate. You will:

- send only a billing phone you are allowed to check
- keep the site key on your server and not publish it
- not use the plugin to harass a person or to collect numbers you do not need for an order

You are responsible for the decision you make on an order after you see the result.

## 5. Availability

Courier services and this website can be unavailable. A failed check is not a delivery history of zero. We may change, pause, or stop the free plugin service.
