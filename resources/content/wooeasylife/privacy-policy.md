# Privacy Policy — WooEasyLife

**Effective date:** June 22, 2026  
**Last updated:** June 22, 2026

## 1. Introduction

WooEasyLife (“**App**”, “**we**”, “**us**”, or “**our**”) is a mobile operations app for WooCommerce merchants who use the **Woo Easy Life** WordPress plugin. The App helps you manage orders, couriers, notifications, fraud checks, and related store operations from your Android device.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, where it is stored, and what choices you have when you use WooEasyLife.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 3. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to:

- The WooEasyLife Android mobile application (`com.wooeasylife.woo_easy_life`)
- Data sent between the App and your connected WordPress store running the Woo Easy Life plugin
- Related services such as push notifications, crash reporting, and optional WPSaleHub-backed features accessed through your store

This policy does **not** govern your WordPress site, WooCommerce store, or third-party courier/SMS providers you configure separately. Those are governed by their own policies and your settings.

## 4. Information we collect

### 4.1 Information you provide

When you connect a store or use App features, you may provide or generate:

- **Store connection details:** WordPress site URL, WordPress username, application password, and license key
- **Store pairing data:** QR pairing code when linking the App to your store
- **Store branding:** Store display name and logo (optional)
- **Firebase configuration (optional):** Firebase service account JSON you paste or import to enable server-side push notifications
- **Notification preferences:** Alert types, sounds, and per-store notification settings
- **Order and customer-related input:** Notes, addresses, order updates, block-list entries, SMS content, and similar merchant-entered data
- **Images or text for order assistance:** Photos or text you submit for optional AI-assisted order extraction features

### 4.2 Information from your connected store

When you use the App, it retrieves data from your WordPress site via the Woo Easy Life plugin, such as:

- Orders, order status, products, and shipping/payment details
- Customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, billing/shipping addresses, and IP addresses where available in orders
- Courier, SMS, analytics, fraud-check, and abandoned-cart data configured in your store
- Call logs and call recordings **if you enable call-tracking features** in the App

This data belongs to your business and is primarily stored on **your WordPress site**, not on our servers as a permanent customer database.

### 4.3 Information collected automatically

The App may automatically collect or generate:

- **Device identifier:** A randomly generated App device ID used for store pairing and API authentication
- **Device information:** Device manufacturer/model label and App version during pairing
- **Network status:** Whether the device has internet connectivity
- **Push notification token (FCM token):** Used to deliver alerts to your device
- **Crash and error reports:** Diagnostic data through Firebase Crashlytics when enabled
- **Local App data:** Onboarding state, notification inbox, cached preferences, and similar data stored on your device

We do **not** use the App to show ads, and we do **not** sell your personal information.

### 4.4 Information collected through device permissions

Depending on features you use, the App may request Android permissions including:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Internet / Network state | Connect to your store and related services |
| Notifications | Show new-order and operational alerts |
| Camera | Scan QR codes to pair your store |
| Phone / Call log / Phone state | Optional call tracking, caller identification, and call duration logging |
| Microphone / Audio recording | **Optional** call recording, only if you turn this feature on |
| Storage / Files | Import notification sounds, Firebase JSON, block lists, and store images |

You can deny or revoke permissions in Android settings, but related features may stop working.

## 5. How we use information

We use collected information to:

- Authenticate and connect the App to your WordPress store
- Display and manage orders, products, couriers, SMS, fraud checks, and dashboard data
- Send and manage push notifications for store events you choose to receive
- Enable optional call tracking, inbound caller identification, and call log syncing
- Upload optional call recordings to your connected store when you enable recording
- Improve App stability and fix crashes
- Provide merchant support and troubleshoot connection issues
- Operate license validation and WPSaleHub-backed features you configure in your store

We process data only as needed to provide the App and related services.

---

## 6. Where data is stored and processed

Data may be stored or processed in the following places:

### On your device

Sensitive credentials (store URL, WordPress credentials, license key) are stored using **encrypted secure storage** on your Android device. Other preferences may be stored in local device storage.

### On your WordPress site

Most business data—orders, customer details, call logs, recordings, SMS history, and store settings—is stored on **your own website/server** through the Woo Easy Life plugin. You control retention and access through your WordPress admin.

### Third-party service providers

**Google Firebase (Google LLC)**

Used for:

- Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications)
- Firebase Crashlytics (crash/error reporting)

Firebase may process device and diagnostic data according to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**WPSaleHub API**

Certain features (such as fraud/delivery checks, SMS, courier integrations, and license services) may be accessed through your WordPress store's proxy to `api.wpsalehub.com`. In those cases, relevant request data (for example, phone numbers for fraud checks) is processed to provide the requested feature.

## 7. How we share information

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share information only in these situations:

- **With your connected store:** The App sends and receives data needed to operate your store features
- **With service providers:** Such as Google Firebase, solely to operate notifications and crash reporting
- **For legal reasons:** If required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security
- **With your direction:** When you choose to use integrations such as courier apps, phone dialer, browser, WhatsApp, or similar external services

Customer and order data you access through the App is shared **to and from your own store**, not to unrelated third parties for marketing.

## 8. Data retention

- **On your device:** Stored until you remove the store, clear App data, or uninstall the App
- **On your store:** Retained according to your WordPress/Woo Easy Life settings and your own policies
- **Firebase data:** Retained according to Google/Firebase settings and our configuration
- **Crash logs:** Retained for a limited period for debugging, then deleted or anonymized per Firebase practices

You are responsible for retention and deletion of customer data on your WooCommerce store.
